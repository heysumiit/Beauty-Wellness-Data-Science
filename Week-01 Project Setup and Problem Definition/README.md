# Week 01 — Project Setup & Problem Definition

### Data Science with Python Internship | Beauty & Wellness

## Overview

Week 1 focused on understanding the Beauty & Wellness industry from a data science perspective and defining a suitable business problem for the project **“Exploring Customer Feedback and Product Sentiment.”**

The objective was to establish the project scope, identify relevant research questions, and outline an initial approach for analyzing customer feedback and product ratings using Python-based data science techniques.

## Objectives

- Understand the business context of the Beauty & Wellness industry.
- Define a specific business problem related to customer feedback and product sentiment.
- Identify research questions and possible hypotheses.
- Determine potential data variables required for analysis.
- Develop an initial plan for data inspection, preprocessing, and exploratory analysis.
- Document the problem statement and project objectives.

## Problem Statement

Beauty & Wellness products receive customer reviews and ratings that can provide insights into product experiences and customer satisfaction. However, these reviews need to be systematically examined to identify useful patterns and trends.

This project aims to explore customer feedback and product ratings to understand review patterns and investigate how text-based information may support customer sentiment analysis.

## Proposed Approach

1. Understand the project requirements and business context.
2. Identify relevant customer-review data and its important fields.
3. Inspect the dataset structure and data quality.
4. Prepare the data for analysis through cleaning and preprocessing.
5. Explore customer ratings and review patterns using visualizations.
6. Investigate suitable approaches for customer feedback and rating analysis.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Visual Studio Code
- Git and GitHub

## Expected Deliverables

- Project problem definition and objectives.
- Initial research questions and analysis plan.
- Python environment setup and verification.
- Week 1 report and supporting documentation.

## Outcome

Week 1 established the foundation for the Beauty & Wellness customer feedback project by defining its purpose, scope, and proposed analytical workflow. These initial decisions support the dataset inspection and data-quality assessment planned for Week 2.
